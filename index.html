Meet Your Coach

Damion Jones

Founder | Recovery & Life Coach | Community Advocate

Real Experience. Real Connection. Real Growth.

I believe that your past does not have to define your future. My journey has been shaped by professional experience, personal growth, community involvement, and a genuine passion for helping people discover their strengths.

Through my work as an Addictions Worker at Nayoskan Detox and Treatment Centre, I've gained professional experience supporting individuals navigating substance use challenges, recovery, and major life transitions.

I founded and led Breaking the Chains, a community initiative that delivered more than 70 sessions with over 300 participant attendances.

My leadership involvement with the ManKind Project, including serving as Alberta Centre Director, has strengthened my commitment to accountability, personal growth, and supportive communities.

Why I Created Rise 'N Recover

I believe everyone deserves a space where they can be heard without judgment, challenged without shame, and supported without being defined by their past.

My approach is compassionate, straightforward, and practical. Together, we focus on building confidence, developing healthier routines, establishing accountability, and taking meaningful steps toward personal goals.

Your past doesn't define your next move. You do.
